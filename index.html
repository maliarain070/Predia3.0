Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Keep this tab open and visible while recording. If your video has no voice, switch to "My own voice" and read the slides aloud, or pick a Google or Microsoft voice in the list. Short paragraphs make better slides.

Questions

Is the video really MP4?

Recent versions of Chrome and Edge record MP4 directly. In older browsers or Firefox the file is WebM. After recording, the page tells you which format you got. Any free converter can turn WebM into MP4.

Why is there no voice in my video?

Browsers cannot save the computer voice directly, so the tool records the tab's audio. Make sure you pick "This tab" and enable "Share tab audio". Some system voices are not captured. Try a different voice or use the microphone option.

Does it render my HTML design exactly?

No. It reads the headings and text from your HTML and shows them as 3D slides. Scripts, styles and images are ignored.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser on your computer.

Does it work on a phone?

Preview works, but recording is most reliable on desktop Chrome or Edge.
